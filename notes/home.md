# Stepwise

Notes on papers in machine learning and information retrieval.

Pick a note from the sidebar. There are two sections: **Information Retrieval** and **Classical ML Models**.

The current reading track is **Information Retrieval**: 29 papers in five parts, from classical ranking to agentic retrieval.

1. **Foundations.** BM25, learning-to-rank, click models, unbiased LTR, online evaluation.
2. **Neural ranking & representation learning.** Two-tower models, cross-encoders, dense/sparse/late-interaction retrieval, ANN.
3. **Landmark industrial systems.** YouTube, Facebook, and Airbnb, plus generative retrieval.
4. **LLMs as labelers, judges & rankers.** LLM relevance labels, the judgment debate, synthetic data, listwise reranking.
5. **RAG & agentic retrieval.** REALM/RAG, long-context behavior, contextual retrieval, search agents.

**Classical ML Models:** linear and regularized regression, logistic regression, SVMs, decision trees, bagging and boosting.
